## Purpose and permission

Tune Into Her supplies general relationship-support ideas and estimated cycle context.
It cannot know another person's feelings. Estimates are based on information entered
by the user and can be inaccurate. It is not a diagnostic, fertility, contraception,
pregnancy-planning, treatment or emergency service. Educational information does not
replace professional health advice.

Only enter another person's cycle information with their permission. Respect their
privacy and their choice to change or withdraw that permission. Do not use estimates
to pressure, monitor or make decisions for another person.

## Card-free access and voluntary purchases

After setup, the user can explicitly start fourteen consecutive 24-hour periods of
application access. Starting this access does not require a card, app account or
subscription. It does not open a store purchase or create an automatic charge at expiry.
The trial service fixes the known start and end dates once; restarting the app or
clearing local cycle information does not extend that known interval.

An optional monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription provides full access. The
store confirmation sheet shows the actual local price, currency, billing period and
applicable taxes. An annual purchase is billed as one annual amount. Buying before
the card-free period ends can charge immediately; unused card-free time is not a
promise of delayed billing or a second store trial.

Paid subscriptions renew under the selected store's displayed terms until cancelled.
Manage cancellation or plan changes through the original Apple or Google account.
Cancellation normally leaves confirmed paid access until its stated end, subject to
the store's refund, revocation, grace and billing-retry decisions. Refund requests and
any mandatory consumer rights follow the applicable store process and local law.

Restore purchases deliberately from the app using the relevant store account. No
automatic transfer between unrelated anonymous devices or stores is promised. Local
data deletion or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription or delete store
transaction records. Privacy, settings, restore and purchase management remain available
after application access expires.

## Local information and availability

Cycle settings, preferences and guidance/feedback history stay in the app's device
storage. The separate trial and purchase services process access-related identifiers
and records described in the [Privacy notice](PRIVACY.md). Previously verified offline
access is time-limited; an internet connection is required for initial trial verification,
store purchases and some recovery actions. Network failure is not a new trial.

Local reminders depend on notification permission and operating-system delivery. They
are general reminders, not health alarms or guaranteed delivery at an exact instant.
Backups, device replacement and operating-system settings affect local data retention.
