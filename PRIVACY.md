This application is a local-first informational relationship tool. Cycle settings entered by the user are stored on the device. They are not sent to the application owner, RevenueCat, or a custom backend. The application has no user account, advertising, behavioral analytics, location collection, contact collection, sale of personal data, or cloud synchronization.

Apple App Store or Google Play processes subscription purchases under its own terms and privacy notice. RevenueCat receives information needed to normalize purchase and subscription status, such as an anonymous app user identifier, product and transaction information, entitlement status, platform, and related technical purchase data. The application does not attach cycle dates, cycle lengths, period lengths, cue history, partner identity, contacts, or location to RevenueCat requests.

Cycle settings use device secure storage where supported. Preferences and non-sensitive cue-selection history use application storage. Removing the application normally removes local application data, subject to operating-system backup behavior and platform rules. Store purchase history remains controlled by the relevant Apple or Google account so purchases can be restored.

Local notifications contain the estimated day and daily cue selected on the device. They are scheduled locally and no push-notification server is used. Users can disable reminders in the application or operating-system settings.
