## Technical help

Describe the screen and action that failed, your app version and operating-system
version. Restart the app and retry when a connection is available. A network error does
not mean that a payment was made. Check the original store's purchase history before
trying to purchase again; use the app's restore and subscription-management actions.

The card-free fourteen-day access starts only when you explicitly choose to start it.
It does not require a card or automatically create a paid subscription at expiry.

## Cancellation, refund and restoration

Manage a native subscription in the Apple or Google account used for purchase. Removing
the app or its local data does not cancel billing. Refund decisions belong to the
original store under its applicable rules. Restoring requires the relevant store account;
unrelated anonymous identities are not automatically linked.

## Privacy and deletion requests

Use the monitored privacy channel once the owner has supplied it. Local cycle/history
removal in Settings affects device data; it does not erase trial records or store and
RevenueCat transactions. No remote self-service trial deletion endpoint is implemented.
The provider must verify ownership safely and handle provider retention/deletion under
the approved privacy process. Never publish trial credentials, restoration links or
transaction tokens to prove ownership. The owner must establish a secure request process
before this page is released; no request mailbox or response deadline is invented here.

Do not send another person's cycle information, private screenshots, payment card data,
passwords, signing keys or authentication credentials. This application and its support
channel do not provide emergency care, diagnosis or treatment.
